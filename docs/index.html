AI chart review

Optional: a vision model reviews your chart for color accessibility and returns structured findings. Your chart is uploaded for analysis only after you approve it in a consent dialog. The corrected copy above is already generated — this adds model commentary alongside measured checks.

Send your chart for AI analysis?

Your chart PNG will be uploaded to the AI provider configured on the ChartSafe backend (an NVIDIA-hosted vision model). It is processed in memory by the provider and is not stored by ChartSafe. Nothing is uploaded until you approve.

Simulations are approximations and vary from individual experience. *The blue-yellow view uses a severe tritanomaly approximation. A recolored image is not a certification of accessibility.
